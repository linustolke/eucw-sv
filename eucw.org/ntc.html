NTC: Netherlands Telegraphy Club
    The goal is to promote, foster and preserve Morse Code. It is an open club with emphasis on Dutch speaking radio amateurs. Club email is pi4ntc at pi4ntc dot nl. Meeting frequencies are 3568 kHz and 7038 kHz. The NTC organizes a QSO party every third Thursday of the month at 19:00 UTC for one hour.
